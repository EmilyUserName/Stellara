The Sign

About Capricorn

Capricorn is the tenth sign of the zodiac — the architect, the executive, the one who builds things that last. Ruled by Saturn, Capricorn energy is disciplined, strategic, and built for the long game. The Sea Goat climbs because it can.

A Capricorn daily horoscope speaks to your ambition, your discipline, and your extraordinary ability to build what you set your mind to. When Saturn steadies your climb, progress is inevitable. The challenge is allowing rest and play alongside the work.

Capricorn Traits

  • Disciplined ambition — knows how to delay gratification for lasting results
  • Long-range thinking — always aware of where the path leads
  • Calm under pressure — their best work comes in difficult conditions
  • Strategic instinct — sees the board several moves ahead
  • Dry wit and understated humor that emerges when you least expect it
