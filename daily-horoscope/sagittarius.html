About Sagittarius

Sagittarius is the ninth sign of the zodiac — the explorer, the philosopher, the one always aiming higher. Ruled by Jupiter, Sagittarius energy is expansive, optimistic, and relentlessly free. The Archer draws the bow before it even knows the target.

A Sagittarius daily horoscope speaks to your hunger for experience, your infectious optimism, and your conviction that something better is always just over the horizon. When Jupiter expands your reach, opportunities multiply. The challenge is following through once the thrill of beginning fades.

Sagittarius Traits

  • Infectious optimism and an unshakeable belief in possibility
  • Adventurous spirit that seeks experience over security
  • Philosophical curiosity — always asking the bigger questions
  • Natural teacher — loves sharing what they've discovered
  • Honest to a fault — prefers a difficult truth over a comfortable lie
