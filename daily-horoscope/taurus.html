The Sign

About Taurus

Taurus is the second sign of the zodiac — the builder, the gardener, the one who turns vision into reality. Ruled by Venus, the planet of beauty and pleasure, Taurus energy is grounded, sensual, and deeply patient. The Bull knows what it wants and is willing to work for it.

A Taurus daily horoscope speaks to your need for security, your love of beauty, and your loyalty to what you value most. When Venus is with you, abundance flows. When the sky pushes, the challenge is to stay rooted without becoming rigid.

Taurus Traits

  • Steadfast loyalty — once committed, rarely wavers
  • Deeply sensual appreciation for beauty, comfort, and pleasure
  • Patient and deliberate — moves at its own pace
  • Practical capability — excellent at making things real and tangible
  • Quiet strength — rarely the loudest in the room, always the most grounded
