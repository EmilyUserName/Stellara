Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Overview

Stellara ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using Stellara, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account or use our services:

  • Account information: email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth)
  • Birth data: date of birth, time of birth (optional), and birth city — used to calculate your astrological chart
  • Name: your first name or preferred name, used to personalize your readings
  • Payment information: processed entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details
  • Reading preferences: your preferred reading style (e.g. Psychological, Spiritual)
  • Email preferences: whether you have opted out of daily emails

How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate your natal birth chart and astrological placements
  • To generate personalized daily readings, solar return forecasts, and other content
  • To deliver your daily reading to your inbox (Pro subscribers)
  • To manage your account, subscription, and purchases
  • To send transactional emails related to your account (e.g. password reset)

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Stellara uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — secure database and authentication storage
  • Stripe — payment processing (we never store payment card data)
  • Resend — email delivery for daily readings and account emails
  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI-generated personalized reading content
  • Netlify — website hosting and serverless functions

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and data handling practices.

Data Retention

We retain your account and birth data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at hello@stellara-horoscope.com.

Cookies & Local Storage

Stellara uses browser local storage to maintain your session and remember your sign-in state. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

Children's Privacy

Stellara is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@stellara-horoscope.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of Stellara after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@stellara-horoscope.com.
